Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026.

CarWorld has no accounts, and it doesn't collect or store your personal information on a server of its own. Here is what happens to your information when you use the site.

Your searches

The details you enter in the search form are used in your browser to build links. They aren't sent to CarWorld. Your browser keeps your most recent search, and any searches you choose to save, in its local storage on your device. Clearing your site data in your browser deletes them.

VIN lookups

When you check a VIN, your browser sends it directly to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) to get vehicle details and recalls. CarWorld doesn't receive or keep the VIN. NHTSA's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Fonts

The site loads the Overpass font from Google Fonts. When the page loads, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

CarWorld is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Links to other sites

When you open a search on Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, Autotrader, Cars.com, eBay Motors, Google or any other site, you leave CarWorld. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or tracking.

Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, CarWorld may earn a commission at no cost to you. CarWorld doesn't share personal information with affiliate partners beyond what your browser sends when you click a link.

Questions

Email your-email@example.com with any privacy question.

CarWorld isn't affiliated with the sites it links to and doesn't host or verify listings. Inspect any vehicle and confirm details with the seller before you pay.
