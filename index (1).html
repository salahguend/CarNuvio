    Check a car before you call the seller

    Most bad deals show warning signs before you ever see the car.

    Vehicle details and recalls come from the U.S. government's NHTSA database. A clean result doesn't replace a history report or an inspection.

    Red flags in a listing

    • The price is far below similar cars, with a story about why it has to go fast.
    • The seller won't meet in person, or asks for a wire transfer, gift cards or crypto.
    • The seller won't share the VIN or a photo of the title.
    • The title is branded salvage, rebuilt, flood or lemon-law buyback.
    • The odometer doesn't match the wear on the pedals, seat and steering wheel.
    • The photos look like stock images, or show a different place than the seller describes.
    • One seller has several cars for sale and the name on the title isn't theirs.

    Before you pay

    • Run the VIN here, then consider a full history report from Carfax or AutoCheck.
    • Pay a local mechanic for a pre-purchase inspection. It typically costs $100 to $200.
    • Meet in a public place. For private sales, finish the deal at a bank or DMV office.
    • Confirm the title is in the seller's name and has no lien on it.
    • Is the title clean, and is it in your name?
    • Has the car been in an accident or had major repairs?
    • Do you have service records?
    • Why are you selling it?
    • Any warning lights, leaks, or trouble with the A/C, brakes or transmission?
    • How many owners has it had?
    • Can I test drive it and take it to my mechanic?
    • Is the price firm?

    Questions people ask

    Is CarWorld free?

    Yes. There's no account and no fee. Some links may be affiliate links. If you buy through one, CarWorld may earn a commission at no cost to you.

    Does CarWorld sell cars or show listings?

    No. CarWorld doesn't host or verify listings. It opens searches on other sites, and the listings belong to those sites.

    Why do some sites open a Google search?

    CarGurus, Carvana, CarMax and OfferUp don't have a search link we can rely on, so those open a Google search limited to that site. It still gets you to their pages quickly.

    Why doesn't Facebook Marketplace use my ZIP code?

    Marketplace uses the location saved in your Facebook account. Change it on Facebook if the results aren't near you.

    Does CarWorld save my searches?

    Only on your own device, and only your most recent search and any you choose to save. They never leave your browser.

    How do I avoid scams?

    Never pay with gift cards or a wire transfer, meet in a public place, and don't buy a car you haven't seen. The Red flags list above covers the rest.
