About CarWorld

A free tool for people shopping for a used car.

Used cars are spread across many sites, and checking each one by hand takes a long time. CarWorld lets you describe the car once and builds the same search on each site, so you can compare what's out there in minutes.

What CarWorld does

You enter a make, model, budget and location. CarWorld builds a ready-to-use search link for sites like Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace, Autotrader, Cars.com and eBay Motors. It also decodes a VIN and checks it against the U.S. government's recall database, and it includes checklists for spotting scams and questions to ask sellers.

What CarWorld doesn't do

CarWorld doesn't host, collect or verify listings, and it isn't affiliated with the sites it links to. Prices, availability and seller claims are theirs. Always inspect a car and confirm the details with the seller before you pay.

How it's built

CarWorld is a simple static website with no accounts and no database. Vehicle details and recall information come from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). You can read how we handle data on the privacy page.
